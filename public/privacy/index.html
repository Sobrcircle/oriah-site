Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

1. Who We Are

Oriah is operated by Moradi Labs Inc. and related service providers working on our behalf.

  • Privacy contact: oriah@moradilabs.com
  • Support contact: oriah@moradilabs.com

2. Scope

This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and delete personal information when you use the Oriah mobile app, website, support channels, and related services.

3. What Oriah Is Designed To Do

Oriah is a private, rhythm-based faith companion built around prayer, scripture, service, trusted circles, and verified church discovery. Individuals are not intended to be publicly searchable. Verified churches may be publicly discoverable.

4. Information We Collect

  • Account and profile information such as email address, display name, profile photo, bio, and account settings.
  • Community content you create or submit, including prayer requests, encouragement messages, chat messages, reactions, circle memberships, journal entries, notes, and other user-generated content.
  • Church and organization information submitted for verification, including public contact details, website, denomination, admin details, and verification materials.
  • Location data when you explicitly enable location-based features such as finding churches, church check-ins, event pins, or travel-related discovery.
  • Device, app, and diagnostic information such as notification tokens, crash reports, service logs, and security events.
  • Billing and subscription metadata from Apple App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat, or other payment providers if premium features are offered. We do not receive your full payment card number from app stores.

5. Private Journal and Personal Reflections

Oriah is designed so that journal entries and private reflections are visible only to the account holder unless you explicitly choose to share content elsewhere in the service. We use service providers and infrastructure needed to store, secure, sync, and back up that data.

6. How We Use Information

  • Provide core features such as Pray, Word, Serve, circles, chat, journal, church discovery, check-ins, and notifications.
  • Personalize your experience, including scripture presentation, day counters, service reflections, and optional church-following features.
  • Verify churches and administer church profiles.
  • Operate account management, support, moderation, abuse prevention, and fraud detection.
  • Maintain security, investigate incidents, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Improve product quality, stability, and user experience.

7. Discovery and Privacy Defaults

  • Individuals are not intended to be publicly searchable by default.
  • Circles are invitation-based and code-gated.
  • Verified churches may have public profiles, searchable listings, and discoverable events.
  • Church visibility does not include public member lists.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information or run advertising inside Oriah. We share information only as needed to operate the service, including with:

  • Hosting, database, analytics, messaging, and security providers
  • Customer support and infrastructure vendors
  • App stores and subscription providers for billing and entitlement management
  • Mapping, geolocation, and church-verification data sources where applicable
  • Authorities or legal counterparties where required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety

9. Permissions and Device Controls

  • Camera and Photos: for profile images, church media, or other uploads you initiate.
  • Notifications: for prayer activity, messages, account events, and reminders if enabled.
  • Location: only for features you choose to turn on.

You can revoke device permissions in your operating system settings at any time.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Active account data is retained while your account remains active.
  • After a verified deletion request, we aim to remove account data from active systems within 30 days.
  • Encrypted or protected backup copies may remain for up to 35 days before automatic deletion.
  • Security, fraud-prevention, tax, billing, dispute, and legal-hold records may be retained longer where required or reasonably necessary.
  • You may request deletion through the app when available or through /delete-account.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export certain personal information. Contact oriah@moradilabs.com for privacy requests.

12. Security

We use authentication controls, infrastructure safeguards, service access controls, and monitoring designed to protect the service and its data. No system is perfectly secure, but we apply reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards.

13. Children

Oriah is not intended for children under 13, and in some regions the minimum age may be higher. If you believe a child submitted personal information without appropriate permission, contact us and we will investigate.

14. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in Canada, the United States, and other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date when changes are posted.

16. Important Service Disclaimer

Oriah is not a church, pastor, counselor, medical provider, or emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
